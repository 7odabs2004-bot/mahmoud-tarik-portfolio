04 — About me

Curious about
what happens
behind the screen.

I'm Mahmoud, a UI/UX Designer, Odoo Developer, AI Automation enthusiast using n8n, and Graphic Designer.

I started with design, but over time I became interested in understanding how products and business systems actually work behind the interface.

That pushed me to explore Odoo, automation, APIs, AI workflows, and different sides of digital creation.

I like experimenting, building things myself, and turning ideas into something real. I'm still at the beginning of my professional journey, and my goal is simple: keep learning, build real things, solve real problems, and work with people who want to create something useful.

01 / CLARITY

Make it obvious.

Good design reduces friction before it adds decoration.

02 / BUILD

Understand the system.

I enjoy knowing what happens behind the interface.

03 / AUTOMATE

Remove repetition.

Use tools and AI where they create real leverage.
