Odoo 17 · Purchase

RFQ Three-Level Approval

A Purchase module that sends every RFQ through three sequential approvers before it can go to a vendor.

  • Three dedicated security groups, one per approval level
  • Server-side checks that block sending, printing, confirming and direct edits that bypass the approval
  • Editing is restricted during approval, and earlier approvals are cleared if commercial data changes
  • Audit log and rejection history on every RFQ
  • Compatible with the native Purchase states and double validation

06 — About me

Curious about
what happens
behind the screen.

I'm Mahmoud Tarik, a UI/UX designer and Odoo developer who builds business automation with n8n, backed by a graphic design practice.

I started in design and moved into development because I wanted to understand how products and business systems actually work behind the interface. That perspective shapes how I work: interfaces that are clear to use, and systems that are reliable to run.

Today my work covers UI/UX design for web and mobile, custom Odoo modules in Python and XML, AI agent workflows in n8n connected to tools like Telegram, Google Sheets and Odoo CRM, and visual design for brands and social media.

I build projects end to end, from the first sketch to a working system, and I'm looking to work with teams and clients who want to ship something useful.

01 / CLARITY

Make it obvious.

Good design reduces friction before it adds decoration.

02 / BUILD

Understand the system.

I enjoy knowing what happens behind the interface.

03 / AUTOMATE

Remove repetition.

Use tools and AI where they create real leverage.
